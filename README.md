## 🎯 How to Use the Flashcards

### Choosing a Category

When the app opens, you will see buttons for different AWS topics, such as:
- All Services (mix of everything)
- Compute (like EC2 and Lambda)
- Storage (like S3 and EBS)
- Security (like IAM and Shield)
- Databases (like RDS and DynamoDB)

Click any button to start a practice session for that category.

### Flipping a Card

Each card shows a question or a term on the front. Click anywhere on the card to flip it and see the answer. Take your time to think before checking.

### Marking Your Answer

After flipping, the app usually asks if you knew the answer or not (buttons like "I knew it" or "Got it wrong"). Be honest! This helps the app track your weak spots.

### Viewing Results

At the end of a session (when you've gone through all cards or a set number), you will see a results screen. It shows your percentage score, how many you got right, and possibly a list of cards you missed. Use this to know what to review again.

### Starting Over

There is always a button to restart or go back to the main menu. You can practice as many times as you like, and the order of cards may change to keep you sharp.

## 💡 Study Tips for the CLF-C02 Exam

- **Regular Short Sessions:** Study for 15-20 minutes daily rather than 2 hours once a week. Repetition is your friend.
- **Mix Categories:** Do not just study one topic. The exam covers a broad range. Use the "All Services" mode often.
- **Read the Explanations:** If a card has extra text or a "More Info" button, read it. Understanding *why* is better than memorizing.
- **Track Your Progress:** Write down your scores in a notebook or use the app's built-in history. You should see improvement over time.
